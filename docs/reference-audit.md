## Product decisions

The core transaction is a submission reviewed into a discoverable permanent listing. Launchdeck puts browsing and search on one screen. Founders supply seven fields and track review with a secret link. Moderators can publish immediately or send a reason back for revision. The marketplace supports organic ranking by recency or votes, with one separate editorial spotlight.

Keep the first release free. Future monetization could use clearly labeled sponsored placement with a scheduling limit and conversion tracking, once organic engagement is measurable. The dependencies for sales are verified billing, disclosure, inventory control, refunds, and reconciliation. Paid entry would still require moderation. Badge verification, AI enrichment, directory syndication and weekly competition each add separate operational work and are deferred.
